{% if LANGUAGE_CODE == 'en' or request.GET.lang == 'en' %} BPH is a consulting company specializing in understanding and management of natural resources. {% if LANGUAGE_CODE == 'en' or request.GET.lang == 'en' %} We commit to reduce environmental impact and to minimizing waste and pollution from our activities.
